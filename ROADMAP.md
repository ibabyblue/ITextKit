# Roadmap

ITextKit currently provides plain and attributed rotation, marquee movement, one-shot typewriter presentation, native text shimmer, and vector styled text with linear-gradient fill and outlines for SwiftUI and UIKit.

The unreleased source also provides an editable image canvas with floating pictures, movement,
rotation, rectangular text wrapping, and persistent document geometry in both frameworks.

## Planned

No additional capabilities are currently planned. Future capabilities will be documented here before placeholder interfaces are introduced.
